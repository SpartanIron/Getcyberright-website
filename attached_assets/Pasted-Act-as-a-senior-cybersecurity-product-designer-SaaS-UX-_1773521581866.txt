Act as a senior cybersecurity product designer, SaaS UX architect, conversion strategist, and full-stack React engineer.

Your task is to design and build the **homepage and UI component system** for a cybersecurity platform called **GetCyberRight**.

The platform helps everyday people protect themselves from:

• phishing scams
• fake job offers
• romance scams
• identity theft
• crypto fraud
• impersonation attacks
• data broker exposure

The product includes:

• Scam Checker
• Data Broker Defense
• Cyber Safety Academy
• AI Safety Tools
• Plain-language security guides

The site must feel like a **serious cybersecurity platform**, not a blog or generic landing page.

The design should combine:

• the structured credibility of enterprise cybersecurity sites
• modern SaaS UI patterns
• clear product interaction
• consumer-friendly accessibility

Do NOT produce a generic marketing site.

---

PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

The homepage must immediately communicate:

1. what the platform does
2. the threat it protects against
3. the tools available
4. why the user should trust it
5. the action the user should take immediately

The homepage must feel like a **cyber defense platform interface**, not just a marketing page.
