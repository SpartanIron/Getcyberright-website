Replace the existing homepage carousel label “Trusted by Organizations Worldwide” with “Trusted Sources” and rebuild the carousel content to reflect mission-aligned credibility rather than corporate logos.

--------------------------------------------------
CAROUSEL PURPOSE
--------------------------------------------------

The “Trusted Sources” carousel should:
- Signal credibility and authority
- Emphasize family safety, education, and consumer protection

CONTENT REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------

  “Trusted by Organizations Worldwide”

  “Trusted Sources”

--------------------------------------------------
SOURCE RESEARCH & CURATION
--------------------------------------------------

- Curate a diverse list of respected organizations from:
  • United States
  • International institutions

VISUAL & UX REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------
