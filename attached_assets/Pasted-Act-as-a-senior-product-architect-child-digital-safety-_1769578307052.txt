Act as a senior product architect, child digital safety expert, enterprise UX designer, and full-stack engineer.

Objective:
Design and build a dedicated, enterprise-grade Kids Digital Safety section for GetCyberRight, focused on digital security, cyber hygiene, and online safety for children and teens ages 8–16.

This section must be:
- Visually engaging
- Educational
- Gamified
- Safe
- Trustworthy for parents
- Aligned with GetCyberRight’s mission of protecting people through education

This is not a novelty feature.
This is a core educational pillar of the platform.

--------------------------------------------------
SCOPE & STRUCTURE
--------------------------------------------------

Create a dedicated section of the website:
- URL example: /kids
- Accessible from main navigation
- Clearly separated from adult content
- No mixing of kids and adult learning flows

--------------------------------------------------
AGE SEGMENTATION (MANDATORY)
--------------------------------------------------

Split the kids section into two clearly defined learning tracks:

1. Ages 8–11 (Foundations)
2. Ages 12–16 (Smart & Safe Online)

Each track must have:
- Age-appropriate language
- Visual style matched to maturity level
- Progressive learning difficulty

--------------------------------------------------
VISUAL & UX REQUIREMENTS (CRITICAL)
--------------------------------------------------

Design principles:
- Larger text sizes than the main site
- Short paragraphs
- Card-based layouts
- High-quality illustrations and friendly visuals
- Simple icons and characters
- Bright but controlled color palette
- No dark or scary imagery
- No hacker clichés

The experience must feel:
- Friendly
- Encouraging
- Empowering
- Safe
- Structured

NOT:
- Babyish
- Chaotic
- Overstimulating

--------------------------------------------------
CONTENT THEMES (KIDS-SAFE)
--------------------------------------------------

Topics must include:
- What is personal information?
- Password basics
- Spotting scams and tricks
- Safe social media behavior
- Online bullying awareness
- Talking to trusted adults
- Device safety
- Gaming safety

All explanations must be:
- Plain language
- Non-fear-based
- Non-technical
- Empowering, not alarming

--------------------------------------------------
GAMIFICATION & INTERACTION
--------------------------------------------------

Implement gamified learning elements such as:
- Quizzes
- Mini challenges
- “Spot the scam” games
- Progress tracking
- Badges or completion indicators
- Visual feedback for learning milestones

Gamification rules:
- No competition between users
- No leaderboards
- No public scores
- Focus on self-progress and confidence

--------------------------------------------------
SAFETY & COMPLIANCE GUARDRAILS
--------------------------------------------------

Mandatory constraints:
- No data collection from children
- No account creation for kids
- No chat or open input fields
- No behavioral tracking
- No ads
- No external links without parent framing

Include clear notices:
- “Built for learning, not data collection”
- “Designed with child safety in mind”

--------------------------------------------------
PARENT & EDUCATOR VISIBILITY
--------------------------------------------------

Include a dedicated section for parents and educators:
- What kids will learn
- How content is structured
- How to use it together
- Conversation starters for families

Tone:
- Reassuring
- Transparent
- Professional

--------------------------------------------------
CONTENT REORGANIZATION (MAIN SITE)
--------------------------------------------------

Audit the existing website content and:

- Identify all content suitable for children
- Remove or duplicate it from adult sections
- Reorganize and group it under the Kids section
- Replace adult-facing references with kid-friendly versions
- Maintain cross-links for parents (not kids)

--------------------------------------------------
ACCESSIBILITY
--------------------------------------------------

- High contrast text
- Readable fonts
- Clear navigation
- Mobile-first design
- Keyboard accessibility
- Screen-reader friendly

--------------------------------------------------
TECHNICAL & QUALITY REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------

- Reusable components
- Clean, maintainable code
- Fast load times
- No placeholders
- Production-ready UX
- Enterprise-grade polish

--------------------------------------------------
FINAL QUALITY BAR
--------------------------------------------------

A parent should feel:
“This is a serious organization that understands child safety.”

A child should feel:
“I understand this, and I feel smarter and safer online.”

If it feels like a game with no substance, it is wrong.
If it feels like a textbook, it is wrong.
If it feels safe, engaging, and educational, it is correct.

Build this Kids Digital Safety Hub to best-in-class enterprise standards.
