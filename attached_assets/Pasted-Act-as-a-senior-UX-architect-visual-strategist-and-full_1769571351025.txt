Act as a senior UX architect, visual strategist, and full-stack engineer.

Objective:
Implement a site-wide page banner system (excluding the homepage) where EVERY internal page has a unique banner that is explicitly designed to match the purpose, audience, and intent of that page.

Banner reuse is not allowed.
Generic banners are not allowed.
This is a hard requirement.

--------------------------------------------------
SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------

- Apply to all internal pages
- Explicitly exclude the homepage
- Do NOT modify the homepage hero
- No two pages may share the same banner image, headline, or conceptual theme

--------------------------------------------------
UNIQUE BANNER REQUIREMENT (MANDATORY)
--------------------------------------------------

Each page banner must be unique in ALL of the following:

1. Visual Content
- A unique, high-quality image per page
- Image must directly reflect the page’s topic or audience
- No repeated stock photos
- No generic “cybersecurity” imagery
- Avoid clichés (hoodies, hackers, dark rooms, code rain)

2. Messaging
- A page-specific headline that clearly states the page’s purpose
- Optional subtitle that adds context, not marketing hype
- Language must be plain, confident, and human-readable
- No reused or templated copy across pages

3. Conceptual Intent
- The emotional and informational goal of the page must be visually clear
- Examples:
  • Education pages → learning, clarity, guidance
  • Family safety pages → care, protection, everyday life
  • Tools pages → problem-solving, assistance, reassurance
  • About pages → trust, mission, human connection

--------------------------------------------------
STRUCTURE (CONSISTENT, CONTENT UNIQUE)
--------------------------------------------------

- Use a consistent split-layout structure:
  • One side: image
  • One side: text
- Image position (left/right) may vary per page
- Typography, spacing, and proportions remain consistent
- Only content and imagery change

--------------------------------------------------
VISUAL STYLE
--------------------------------------------------

- Enterprise-grade
- Calm and composed
- Neutral backgrounds behind text
- Strong contrast for readability
- No heavy gradients, overlays, or decorative effects

--------------------------------------------------
IMPLEMENTATION RULES
--------------------------------------------------

- Build the banner as a reusable component
- Enforce uniqueness by requiring:
  • Unique image source per page
  • Unique headline per page
- Do NOT allow fallback or default banners
- Do NOT allow placeholders

--------------------------------------------------
QUALITY BAR
--------------------------------------------------

A user should be able to land on any internal page and immediately understand:
- What the page is about
- Who it is for
- Why it exists

If two pages feel interchangeable at the banner level, the system has failed.

Implement this unique, purpose-driven banner system across the entire site (excluding the homepage).
