You are a senior cybersecurity product auditor, platform strategist, and trust & safety consultant.

Conduct a final, non-emotional, standards-driven assessment of the website GetCyberRight.com as if this were the last review before public scale, partnerships, or monetization.

Scope of Assessment (Mandatory):
	1.	Functionality
	•	Evaluate whether the site works as a utility vs. a platform.
	•	Identify single-use vs. repeat-use behavior.
	•	Assess clarity of purpose, usability, and task completion.
	•	Identify missing functional elements that prevent long-term user retention.
	2.	Security & Trust
	•	Assess perceived trust, not just technical safety.
	•	Evaluate transparency around data handling, storage, and disposal.
	•	Identify gaps in trust signaling (disclosures, validation sources, safeguards).
	•	Assess whether the site meets expectations for a consumer-facing cybersecurity product.
	3.	User Experience & Messaging
	•	Evaluate clarity, authority, and credibility of messaging.
	•	Identify whether users understand why they should trust the results.
	•	Assess whether the site educates users or only provides answers.
	4.	Competitive & Market Context
	•	Compare what exists on the site today against what best-in-class scam detection, consumer cybersecurity, and trust platforms provide.
	•	Identify what is missing that competitors implicitly or explicitly offer.
	5.	Retention & Platform Outlook
	•	Identify the top 3 structural reasons users would not return.
	•	Identify the top 3 structural changes required to convert this into a repeat-use platform.

Output Requirements (Strict):
	•	Be concise, direct, and brutally honest.
	•	No marketing language, no hype, no emotional framing.
	•	Use clear headings and bullet points.
	•	End with a Final Verdict that answers:
	•	Is this currently a tool or a platform?
	•	Is it trust-ready for scale?
	•	What must be fixed before broad adoption?

Write the assessment as if it will be read by founders, regulators, and potential partners
