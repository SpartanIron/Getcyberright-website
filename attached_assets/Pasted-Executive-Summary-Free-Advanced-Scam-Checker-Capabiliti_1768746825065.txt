Executive Summary: Free, Advanced Scam Checker Capabilities

You can significantly differentiate GetCyberRight’s scam checker without any paid tools by implementing the following core capabilities using open-source models and public data.

1. Intelligent Scam Reasoning (Text-Based)

Analyze messages, emails, and chats using open-source AI to identify social engineering tactics (urgency, authority, fear, payment pressure) and explain why something is likely a scam—not just label it.

2. Zero-Day Scam Detection

Use anomaly detection on message structure and language to flag new or emerging scam patterns before they appear on blacklists.

3. Scam Psychology Identification

Classify manipulation techniques being used (fear, scarcity, impersonation, emotional bonding) to educate users and increase trust in the result.

4. Free Link & Domain Risk Analysis

Assess URLs using public data (domain age, certificates, DNS behavior, homograph detection) to provide infrastructure-level risk context without paid threat feeds.

5. Screenshot & Image Scam Analysis

Allow users to upload screenshots; extract text via OCR and analyze it for scam patterns, fake branding, and embedded links—an area most scam checkers ignore.

6. Scam Template Matching

Detect reused scam templates using semantic similarity, even when wording changes, to catch evasive scams.

7. Explainable Risk Scoring

Provide a transparent risk score with clearly listed contributing factors to build user trust and credibility.

8. Actionable Safety Guidance

Go beyond detection by telling users exactly what to do next (do not respond, block, report, contact bank, preserve evidence).

9. Scam Trend Tracking

Anonymously track scam patterns over time to identify and publish emerging scam trends, increasing platform authority.

10. Privacy-First Design

Operate without storing personal data by default, making the tool trustworthy and compliant without regulatory complexity.
