Conduct a comprehensive audit of the GetCyberRight website to identify and catalog all instances of data redundancy, duplicate content, and information overload that may be creating friction in the user journey from awareness to signup/engagement.

## Business Context
GetCyberRight is a family-focused cyber fraud protection platform targeting:
- **Primary audience**: Parents, guardians, and family decision-makers across US, European, and Asian markets
- **Core value proposition**: Making cybersecurity accessible and actionable for families
- **Competitive positioning**: Family-centric approach vs. enterprise-focused competitors
- **User journey goals**: Education → Trust → Conversion (signup/download)

## Scope of Analysis

### 1. Content Redundancy Audit
- **Messaging overlap**: Identify where the same value propositions, benefits, or features are repeated across multiple pages
- **Educational content**: Map duplicate explanations of cyber threats, fraud types, or protection strategies
- **Origin story/mission**: Check for repetitive founder narrative or company mission statements
- **Trust signals**: Identify redundant testimonials, credentials, or security assurances
- **CTA language**: Catalog similar or identical calls-to-action competing for attention

### 2. Structural & Navigation Analysis
- **Page hierarchy**: Identify redundant service/feature pages or overlapping category structures
- **Navigation paths**: Map multiple routes to the same information (header, footer, sidebar, in-content links)
- **Landing pages**: Check for duplicate landing page variants targeting similar audiences
- **Resource pages**: Identify overlapping blog posts, guides, FAQs, or educational materials covering the same topics

### 3. Family-Focused UX Considerations
- **Information density**: Assess whether technical complexity is overwhelming non-technical family users
- **Decision paralysis**: Identify too many feature options or service tiers without clear guidance
- **Trust-building redundancy**: Determine if security messaging is over-emphasized to the point of creating anxiety rather than confidence
- **Multi-generational clarity**: Check if content is duplicated to serve different age groups instead of being consolidated with clear segmentation

### 4. Technical Asset Duplication
- **Images/graphics**: Identify duplicate hero images, icons, or visual assets
- **Forms**: Check for redundant contact forms, signup forms, or lead capture mechanisms
- **Scripts/tracking**: Look for duplicate analytics or tracking implementations
- **Pop-ups/modals**: Identify competing or redundant overlay CTAs

### Phase 2: Strategic Recommendations

**Merge Candidates**
- Which pages/sections should be consolidated
- Proposed unified structure
- Content hierarchy within merged page
- Expected user journey improvement

**Remove Candidates**

- Outdated or conflicting information
- Low-value duplicate pages

**Consolidate & Enhance**
- Content that should be combined and expanded
- Opportunities to create comprehensive resource pages
- Navigation simplification strategies

**Redirect Strategy**
- SEO-safe redirect mapping for removed pages
- Internal link updates required
- External link preservation plan

### Phase 3: Prioritized Action Plan

- **Impact vs. Effort scoring** for each recommendation

- User behavior change considerations
- Brand messaging consistency implications

**Implementation Sequence**
