# GetCyberRight - Business Overview

## Executive Summary

**GetCyberRight** is an always-on family cyber safety service platform helping families stay safe online across six continents. Built from personal experience with cyber fraud, we provide cyber safety resources for every family: from kids on social media to seniors and their families targeted by scammers.

**Model**: Mission-driven service platform with productized feature modules

**Mission**: "Helping families stay safe online, from kids on social media to seniors targeted by scammers."

**Contact**: hello@getcyberright.com

## Business Model

### Service Platform Positioning

GetCyberRight operates as a **service platform**, not a standalone product. This positioning:
- Matches our free, trust-first brand identity
- Creates natural partnership and advertising opportunities
- Allows continuous content evolution without version updates
- Builds authentic audience relationships through ongoing value delivery

### Productized Modules

Within the service platform, we offer defined feature modules that satisfy partner and investor expectations:

| Module | Description | Value to Partners |
|--------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Interactive Safety Assessments** | Gamified quizzes with points, achievements, streaks | Engagement metrics, sponsored challenges |
| **Real-Time Threat Dashboard** | Live statistics from FBI IC3, INTERPOL, regional sources | Authority positioning, data partnerships |
| **Daily Tips Email System** | Automated Monday/Friday safety tips by audience | Newsletter sponsorship, direct reach |
| **Youth Protection Hub** | Social media, gaming, mobile safety guides | School/parent partnerships |
| **Senior Safety Center** | Scam recognition, mobile money fraud prevention | Financial institution partnerships |

### Mobile App Roadmap

Future mobile apps extend the service platform to new channels:

| Tier | Features | Monetization |
|------|----------|--------------|
| **Free** | Core safety guides, push alerts, daily tips | Ad-supported, partner-sponsored |
| **Premium** | Offline safety kits, family monitoring dashboard, ad-free | Subscription ($2.99-4.99/month) |
| **Enterprise** | White-label for schools/orgs, bulk licensing, custom branding | B2B contracts |

Mobile apps reinforce the service promise (always-on protection) while creating optional premium revenue streams.

## Value Proposition

### The Problem
- Cybercrime losses exceeded $12.5B globally in 2023 (FBI IC3)
- Seniors lose an average of $35,000+ per scam incident
- Youth face unprecedented threats from social media, gaming, and mobile platforms
- Families worldwide lack accessible, culturally relevant cyber safety resources

- **Mobile-first design** optimized for developing markets (75%+ mobile traffic)
- **Real-time threat intelligence** from 20+ trusted sources
- **Geo-personalized experience** with regional headlines and emergency contacts

### Unique Positioning
**The only platform combining Youth Protection + Senior Safety + Real-time News** with mobile-first design serving families globally across six continents.

## Trust & Security

### Platform Security
- **Enterprise-grade headers**: Helmet.js with strict CSP, HSTS, XSS protection
- **Rate limiting**: Multi-tier protection against abuse
- **Bot protection**: Honeypot fields and timing detection
- **Input sanitization**: All user inputs sanitized to prevent injection attacks
- **Responsible disclosure**: security.txt for ethical security researchers

### Content Credibility
- **Authoritative sources**: FBI IC3, INTERPOL, CISA, and regional cybercrime agencies
- **Cited statistics**: All data includes source attribution and report dates
- **Regular updates**: Quarterly refresh of threat statistics
- **No sensationalism**: Factual, actionable guidance without fear-mongering

### Privacy Commitment
- No behavioral tracking or surveillance advertising
- Minimal data collection (email for subscriptions only)
- No data sales to third parties
- GDPR-conscious design principles
