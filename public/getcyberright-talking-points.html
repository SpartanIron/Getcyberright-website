GetCyberRight: Talking Points

Prepared responses for partnership conversations

Origin and Mission

What inspired you to start GetCyberRight?
Personal experience with cyber fraud showed me how devastating it can be for families. I realized that existing cybersecurity resources are too technical for everyday people. Seniors, parents, and young people all need protection, but the advice out there reads like it was written for IT professionals. GetCyberRight exists to give families the same quality protection that corporations have, in language anyone can understand.
What is your core mission?
To make cybersecurity accessible to every family, everywhere. We believe safety online should not depend on technical expertise or ability to pay. Our mission is to bridge the gap between complex security information and practical, everyday protection.

Product and Capabilities

What exactly does your platform do?
We provide free AI-powered tools that help families identify scams before they become victims. Someone can paste a suspicious link, upload a screenshot of a weird message, or simply describe a situation in plain English, and our system analyzes it instantly. We also provide educational content tailored for different audiences: youth, seniors, parents, and travelers. Everything is backed by real threat intelligence from sources like the FBI and INTERPOL.
How is this different from existing security products?
Three key differences. First, our core tools are free, not behind a paywall. Second, we write in plain language that a grandmother or teenager can understand, not technical jargon. Third, we cover global threats including mobile money scams in Africa and regional patterns across six continents. Most competitors focus only on US and European markets.
What technology powers your scam detection?
We use a combination of rule-based analysis and AI. For URLs, we check against known threat databases and analyze patterns that indicate phishing. For more complex situations, we use AI with strict guardrails to prevent false alarms. Our AI only makes claims it can support with evidence, and always references authoritative sources.

Market and Opportunity

How big is the market opportunity?
Cybercrime costs are projected to reach $10.5 trillion annually by 2025. Seniors alone lose over $3 billion to scams each year according to FBI data. Yet most cybersecurity products target enterprises, not families. The consumer protection market is underserved, and underserved communities in Africa, Asia, and Latin America represent a massive untapped opportunity.
Why is now the right time?
AI is making scams more sophisticated and harder to detect. At the same time, more families are online than ever, especially in developing regions. Regulatory pressure around privacy is increasing, which favors platforms like ours that respect user data. The gap between threat sophistication and public awareness is widening, and we are positioned to close it.

Business Model

How do you plan to make money if core tools are free?
We follow a freemium model. Free tools build trust and user base. Premium tiers offer enhanced detection with commercial APIs for power users and small businesses. Enterprise customers like schools, employers, and financial institutions can license white-label solutions. We also see partnership opportunities with telecoms and banks who want to protect their customers.
What would investment or support enable?
Three priorities. First, mobile app development to reach users where they are. Second, premium API integrations that enhance our detection capabilities. Third, marketing to reach underserved communities who need us most. With the right support, we can scale from a working platform to a global safety resource.

Credibility and Trust

How do you ensure your information is accurate?
All our content references authoritative sources: FBI IC3, INTERPOL, CISA, Europol, and regional cybercrime agencies. Our AI has strict anti-hallucination guardrails and only makes claims supported by evidence. We never use fear-mongering or sensationalism. Our users trust us because we earn that trust with every interaction.
How do you handle user privacy?
We collect minimal data, do not track user behavior, do not sell any data, and design with GDPR principles in mind. Privacy is not just a feature; it is core to our mission. Families trust us with sensitive situations, and we honor that trust.
Tip: If asked about specific metrics, you can mention that you track usage analytics for scam checks and subscriber engagement, with full breakdowns by type and region available in your admin portal.

Vision

Where do you see GetCyberRight in five years?
I see us as the trusted first stop for any family facing a potential online threat. Mobile apps in every app store. Partnerships with major telecoms and banks. Educational programs in schools and senior centers. A recognized global brand for family cyber safety, with free tools remaining at the core of what we do.
