# GetCyberRight
## Empowering Families, Seniors, and Businesses with Digital Safety

**Operated by ColorCode Solutions LLC**

---

## Executive Summary

GetCyberRight is a comprehensive cybersecurity education platform designed to protect everyday people from the growing threat of online scams, phishing attacks, and digital fraud. Unlike technical security solutions aimed at IT professionals, GetCyberRight translates complex cybersecurity concepts into actionable, easy-to-understand guidance for families, seniors, parents, educators, and small businesses.

The platform combines AI-powered threat analysis, gamified learning experiences, and automated threat intelligence to keep users informed and protected in real-time.

---

## The Problem We Solve

**Cybercrime is on the rise, and everyday people are the primary targets:**

- The FBI's Internet Crime Complaint Center (IC3) reported over $16.6 billion in losses from cybercrime in 2024
- Seniors lose an estimated $3 billion annually to scams
- Small businesses are targeted in 43% of all cyberattacks
- Phishing remains the most common attack vector, with over 500 million attacks reported annually
- Voice cloning and deepfake scams are emerging as the next major threat

**Current solutions fall short:**

Most cybersecurity resources are too technical for average users, leaving families and small businesses vulnerable. GetCyberRight bridges this gap with plain-language education and practical tools.

### AI-Powered Protection Tools

**Dezi AI Assistant**
Our intelligent assistant analyzes suspicious content and provides instant guidance:
- Email header analysis to detect phishing
- URL safety checking across multiple threat databases
- Voice message analysis for deepfake detection
- Real-time scam pattern recognition

**Security Scanner**
Multi-engine threat detection using:
- Google Safe Browsing API
- VirusTotal integration
- Domain age and reputation checks
- SSL certificate validation
- QR code scanning for hidden threats

### Competitive Differentiators

**Family Safety Codeword Generator**
A unique tool that helps families create secure verification codewords to protect against voice cloning scams. When someone calls claiming to be a family member in distress, the codeword system helps verify their identity.

**Voice Clone Detection**
Upload suspicious voicemails to analyze for AI-generated voice patterns, protecting against the emerging threat of voice cloning fraud.

**Gamification System**
Points, badges, and leaderboards encourage continued learning and security awareness, making digital safety engaging rather than intimidating.

**Regional Threat Intelligence**
Automatic detection of user location to provide locally relevant threat information, scam alerts, and emergency resources.

## Technology Platform

### Enterprise-Grade Security

GetCyberRight practices what it teaches, implementing comprehensive security measures:

- **NIST 800-53 Compliance**: 82% compliance score across 20 control families
- **MITRE ATT&CK Mapping**: 87/100 security coverage score
- **Real-Time Monitoring**: Automated threat detection and incident response
- **Multi-Factor Authentication**: Optional TOTP-based MFA for all user accounts
- **Rate Limiting**: Three-tier protection against automated attacks

### Automated Threat Intelligence

The platform continuously ingests data from 8 trusted sources:

1. URLhaus (Malware URLs)
2. PhishTank (Verified Phishing)
3. CISA Alerts (Government Advisories)
4. NVD (National Vulnerability Database)
5. Have I Been Pwned (Data Breach Monitoring)
6. Google Safe Browsing
7. VirusTotal
8. RDAP/WHOIS (Domain Intelligence)

This automated system ensures educational content stays current with the latest threats.

### AI Content Governance

All AI-generated content undergoes rigorous quality controls:

- **Truthfulness Validation**: All recommendations traced to verified threat data
- **Anti-Hallucination Checks**: Automated detection of potentially fabricated information
- **Writing Quality Standards**: Professional formatting without technical jargon
- **Source Attribution**: All threat-based guidance cites original intelligence sources
