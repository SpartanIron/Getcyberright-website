# GetCyberRight

## The Family Cybersecurity Platform

---

### Mission

GetCyberRight exists to make cybersecurity accessible, actionable, and human. We translate complex digital threats into clear guidance that families, seniors, educators, and small businesses can act on immediately — no technical background required.

---

### The Problem

Cybersecurity has a reach problem. The industry produces excellent threat intelligence, but it stays trapped inside enterprise dashboards, technical blogs, and jargon-heavy advisories. Meanwhile:

- 74% of data breaches involve a human element (Verizon DBIR)
- Seniors lose an estimated $3.4 billion annually to online fraud (FBI IC3)
- Parents report feeling "overwhelmed" by the pace of new digital threats to their children
- Small businesses are targeted in 43% of cyberattacks, yet most lack dedicated security resources

The people who need cybersecurity guidance the most are the least served by the industry.

GetCyberRight is a full-stack cybersecurity education and protection platform. Everything is free. No paywalls, no tiered access, no upsells.

### Technology

**Frontend**: React 19, TypeScript, Next.js App Router, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui
**Backend**: Express 5, Node.js, TypeScript, Drizzle ORM, PostgreSQL
**AI**: Anthropic Claude for content generation, scam analysis, and conversational support
**Security**: Helmet.js, multi-tier rate limiting, CSRF protection, XSS/SQLi detection, AES-256-GCM encryption for the Personal Data Vault, nonce-based Content Security Policy
**Authentication**: Email/password, TOTP MFA, Passkeys (WebAuthn)
**Infrastructure**: ISR-enabled for performance, automated CI/CD, Replit Object Storage for media assets

### Social Media & Content Distribution

GetCyberRight operates an AI-powered social content engine that publishes to LinkedIn, Facebook, and Instagram via Buffer integration.

**Three-Tier Content Depth Model**:

- **Wire** (30-50 words): Breaking news teasers with branded image cards — drives traffic to full articles
- **Insight** (80-120 words): Standalone educational content with authentic editorial photography — builds authority and reach
- **Deep Value** (100-150 words): Actionable how-to content designed for saves and shares — drives organic growth

The system uses a 12-step AI pipeline that selects depth, tone, platform-specific formatting, and image strategy per post. Content quality is enforced by automated policy: word limits, 30+ banned corporate phrases, rotating hook formulas, and curated hashtag strategies designed to reach mainstream audiences beyond the cybersecurity echo chamber.

**Daily output**: Up to 2 posts per platform, per day — each tailored to the platform's algorithm preferences.

### What Makes Us Different

1. **Free for everyone.** No freemium model. No paywalls. Every tool, every guide, every course — free.

2. **Human-first language.** We don't write for security professionals. We write for the parent who just got a suspicious text, the grandmother who isn't sure if that email is real, and the small business owner who doesn't have an IT department.

3. **AI-powered at scale.** From scam analysis to content generation to social distribution, AI handles the heavy lifting so our content stays current with the threat landscape — not weeks behind it.

4. **Government-grade intelligence, consumer-grade delivery.** We aggregate from CISA, US-CERT, FBI IC3, FTC, Europol, and 15+ authoritative sources, then translate it into language anyone can act on.

5. **Full-stack protection.** Not just education — actual tools. Scam analysis, breach monitoring, data broker removal, identity masking, and a browser extension that works in real time.
